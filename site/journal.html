What kind of entry?

Entries are public, with the nickname, device and version you give, and each one is emailed to us. Please don't post personal details. An email address is kept only to answer you; ask at khrecast@gmail.com and we'll delete it, or your entry.

This site keeps no record of you: it only adds to daily counts of visits, downloads, link clicks and which parts of the home page were scrolled to, with no cookies. The counts are by hour, country (as Cloudflare works it out), kind of computer (Windows, Mac, Linux, phone) and the site that linked here (its name only), each kept on its own, and nothing is counted for browsers that send Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. (Cloudflare, which hosts the site, keeps its usual server logs.) The one exception is what you write in Jiminy's Journal: your entry is public and is also emailed to the project inbox, and an email address you leave there is kept privately so we can answer you.
