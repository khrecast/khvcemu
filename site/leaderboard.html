The original ranking server shut down long ago, so Re:Cast runs a replacement. Scores are anonymous: there are no names, only how the run went, when it was shared and the kind of device it was played on. Players who share their scores from the launcher show up here. In the game, the score is your Munny plus 100× your EXP.

Equal scores are ranked by the faster time. Scores and times come from players’ own computers and can’t be verified, so treat this as friendly fun rather than a competition. Scores from runs that used a save state, or that don’t beat a score the player already shared, stay on the player’s own computer and are not shared.

This site keeps no record of you: it only adds to daily counts of visits, downloads, link clicks and which parts of the home page were scrolled to, with no cookies. The counts are by hour, country (as Cloudflare works it out), kind of computer (Windows, Mac, Linux, phone) and the site that linked here (its name only), each kept on its own, and nothing is counted for browsers that send Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.
