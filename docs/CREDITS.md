# Credits

Kingdom Hearts Re:Cast only exists because of people who found, dumped, restored,
recorded and wrote about this game, often years before the emulator. This list
names them and links to where they can be found. Links were checked in
October 2026. Everything here comes from public sources (linked); where a role or
a link could not be confirmed it says so under [Still to confirm](#still-to-confirm).

If you are listed and would like your link changed, added or removed, email
**khrecast@gmail.com**.

## Recovering the game's files

| Who | What they did | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **"Wedge"** (4chan /v/) | Found and shared the first chapter's files (Obstacle Course and Swashbuckler's Island), July 2016. | [KH13 news report](https://www.kh13.com/news/kingdom-hearts-v-cast-code-released-including-various-screenshots-audio-files-more/) |
| **rsznik** (Internet Archive) | Preserved Wedge's upload on the Internet Archive, February 2018. | [Chapter 1 on archive.org](https://archive.org/details/KingdomHeartsVCastChapter1), [the V Cast collection](https://archive.org/details/kh-v-cast-collection) |
| **"Cbajd5"** (KHInsider forums) | Dumped and uploaded the third chapter, Agrabah, October 2018. | [Lost Media Wiki](https://lostmediawiki.com/Kingdom_Hearts:_V_Cast_(partially_lost_mobile_game;_2005)), [KHwiki](https://www.khwiki.com/Kingdom_Hearts_V_CAST) |
| **"Eriyu"** and **"kraze1984"** (KHInsider forums) | Dumped and uploaded the fourth chapter, Maleficent's Castle, 2022. | [Lost Media Wiki](https://lostmediawiki.com/Kingdom_Hearts:_V_Cast_(partially_lost_mobile_game;_2005)), [KHwiki](https://www.khwiki.com/Kingdom_Hearts_V_CAST) |

Chapter 2, *Alice in Wonderland*, has never been recovered. See the
[README](../README.md#wonderland-lost-media) if you can help.

## Look and feel

| Who | What | Links |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Televo** | Recreated the KHMenu font (the Kingdom Hearts start-menu lettering) for the free "Kingdom Hearts Re:Collection" set. The Re:Cast title in the logo and the launcher's tab names are drawn in it. | [Kingdom Hearts Re:Collection](https://github.com/Televo/kingdom-hearts-recollection) |

The Re:Collection set has no formal licence. Its README says: "If you decide to use these assets
and fonts publicly, giving credit is much appreciated although not necessary." This repository
does not contain the font file, only small pictures of the tab names rendered from it
(`khvcemu/assets/tabs/`, made by `tools/make_tab_labels.py`).
